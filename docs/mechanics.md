Mechanics
Active Time Battle System.
Player and enemies have the following stats:
Health / Shield / Active buffs (Healing, poison, etc)

Key; Means nothing. Defined at the beginning of the fight
Chords: Player can select a pre made chord progression, if they wants to swap it mid run, they will have to do it with quick time events and cant do other tasks while doing it. Chords create some kind of effect (not decided yet). In advanced modes, player can unlock the craft of any chord progression combination possible. Only good chords will have relevant effects. A scrip has to run on the custom chords to see if they sound good with quantifiable metrics like:
1.  Voice-Leading Parsimony (Kinetic Cost): How many semitones do voices have to move from chord to chord? (Lower movement = smoother, more cohesive).
2. Diatonic Fit (Key Stability): Can the progression be anchored to a recognizable tonal center (key)?
3. Harmonic Syntax / Flow: Does the progression build and release tension logically (Tonic, subdominant, dominat, tonic)
Players can select a weapon (instrument). They all have unique properties. Playing the notes with wasd will record them in a loop station and reproduce them over and over again. The loop is running since the beginning of the fight. The player input will be recorded in the exact spot where the loop is moving (example the compass has 12 tempos and player press a note when the line is in the #4, it will be inserted at that spot. The notes are attacks and when the player goes over them, the damage will be applied.
Player can carry multiple characters, each with a different weapon, in that way, melodies can be stacked. Each instrument has its own individual loop station, so multiple instruments can run at the same time.
Player can record melodies with the instruments pre fight. To cast a pre made melody  in the battle, user must select it and perform a quick time event like fnf with wasd
Combat flow example:
Player pre selects a chord: ["C", "Am", "F", "G"]
It activates an effect for the rest of the combat and starts playing in the background.
Enemy created a melody and attacks in its turn
Player creates a melody using WASD with violins and it gets recorded in the already running loop, enemy receives slow poison damage from it
Player creates a second melody with a second character. it is a piano. high critic probabilities agains the enemy
Over time, effect of the recorded melodies fade, player has to record new ones until the end of the battle selecting a new preset. Old melodies sets are saved in presets that can be ran back when their cool down ends. creating a new identical preset will be detected and the same cool down will be applied. Deleting the set and creating it to bypass cool down wont be allowed either.

Before combat, Characters can be placed as: Chord maker (will play the chord with the instrument of that player), attackers (1 to 3 instruments to create the melodies that can also overlap or be placed in long sequences), defender (builds the beats, will parry enemy attacks with quick time action using wasd too, this is different to the rest in the sense that the keys dont have to match the actual key. If player prefers songs with no beat, they can place an instrument here, and in the same way, each key pressed wont match the actual key, it will just map an arpeggio that fits with the base chord)
Attackers can build short notes and long ones (auto detect long press and map it to an extended note across the loop timeline. Enemies have more probabilities to evade long note attacks, but if they hit, deal a lot of damage)
When there are multiple enemies at the same time, note attacks will select random targets. Meta must reward short notes against weak hordes, and slow notes for slow tanks, and a mix of both for balanced enemies.
Enemies can be weak/resistant against specific instruments, elements and effects.

Enemies in depth: Their attacks will always be arpeggios that match with the note. They will appear in the quick time event panel and player has to match the rhythm with wasd.
There are variants with special abilities: Reflect the damage of all the player notes that are not part of the chord, or long notes, or short notes. deletes your tracks and player has to remake all their note attacks. Most of them deal at least a fraction of their attack damage if you block it.
Screen:
Left, center and Right section:
- I the right, the player characters with their instrument (The game has unlockable characters, 1 to 3 characters per instrument). Player can move them from the deck to their position, all characters are cards (chord and beat in the "back" position, attackers in the front). 
- In the center of the screen: the loop live grid with the melodies. One grid for each instrument, Stacked one on top of the other.
- A chord wheel with the labels of the chords in the center. Press it to change the chord and perform the Quick time action event in the "DDR-style" or "Piano Roll".
- "DDR-style" or "Piano Roll". This is used to block enemy attacks and to change chords and to insert pre designed melodies
- In the left. Enemy characters. One card per enemy. It displays their image and stats. One card per enemy, the same with player characters
Quick time action screen (for the beats parry when the enemy is attacking or for the quick time events that the player triggered himself like changing a chord progression, it would be like fnf), and in the other corner, the enemies.
